My background spans software development, business systems, project coordination and data-driven initiatives. Over the years, I've worked with private companies, public institutions and nonprofit organizations, taking on different roles depending on what each project needed — from building software and improving operational processes to coordinating implementations and connecting data from different sources.

That variety has shaped how I approach technology: not as an end in itself, but as a way to address real organizational needs. I value understanding the problem first, finding a practical way forward, and making sure the solution works in the context where it will be used.

How I work

Most of my work starts before the technology: understanding how an organization actually operates, what it needs and what a practical solution would look like. Sometimes that means analyzing a process, sometimes coordinating a team, sometimes writing the scripts or the code that bring data together. My background as a developer is what lets me understand, evaluate and implement the solutions I recommend.

I'm especially interested in problems where technology has to adapt to a complex real-world process, not the other way around, and in projects that make organizations more efficient, transparent or accessible.
